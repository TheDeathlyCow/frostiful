# Frostiful 3.1.1 for Mineraft 26.1.x

Some small adjustments and fixes.

- The Warming effect is now canceled once the target is warmed enough.
- The Freezing effect now applies to both warm and cold entities.
- The Frost Bite has been renamed to Frostbite (removed the space).
- The Frostbite effect has been given a new icon.
- Frostbite now reduces Environment Frost Resistance by 25% per level.
- Fixed translation strings for renamed mob effects and potions.
