# Frostiful 3.2: Cranking out more new textures

- Updated the following textures:
  - Packed Snow
  - Packed Snow Bricks
  - Cut Packed Ice
  - Cut Blue Ice
  - Ice Pane
  - Glacial Arrow (item and entity)
- Removed the Freezing effect from the Potion of Numbing
- Replaced the direct freezing from Glacial Arrows with a short application of Freezing
  - This should be stronger than the original amount, but applied as a DOT
  - The old temperature source setting `glacialArrowFreezingEffectDurationMultiplier` will be automatically migrated to the new item setting `glacialArrowFreezingEffectDurationMultiplier`
- Added Fox and Panda fur tuft items
  - Like Polar Bear Fur Tufts, 4 Panda Fur Tufts craft to a Fur Padding
  - Like Wolf Fur Tufts, Ocelot Fur Tufts, and Rabbit hides, 9 Fox Fur Tufts craft to a Fur Padding
- Foxes and Pandas can now play fight to drop fur tufts
  - Baby Pandas will be able to play fight if they are NOT weak or worried variants
  - Adult Pandas will be able to play fight ONLY if they are either aggressive or playful variants
- Foxes and Pandas can now be brushed to drop fur tufts
  - Aggressive pandas will begin to attack their target if they are brushed
- Added a new advancement for brushing every brushable animal, called "Can I pet that dawwwwg??"
- Massively decreased the number of fur tufts dropped from play fighting by all animals
- Fixed brushing cooldowns not correctly applying
- Mitigated baby polar bears becoming decapitated while play fighting
  - Baby polar bears no longer do the standing animation when they playflight
  - The underlying issue is caused by the vanilla bug [MC-145654](<https://bugs.mojang.com/browse/MC/issues/MC-145654>)
- Fixed the general heating/cooling temperature source multipliers not applying to the warming and freezing mob effects
